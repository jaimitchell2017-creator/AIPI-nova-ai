Nova

Desk companion firmware for the XORIGIN AIPI Lite. Phase 1: clock and weather.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Chromebook. This page must be opened over https.
  1. Plug the AIPI Lite into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Install Nova and pick the serial port that appears.
  3. If no port appears or flashing fails, put the device in bootloader mode (see the README) and try again.
  4. After flashing, the screen shows a test pattern, then asks you to join the Wi-Fi called Nova-Setup on your phone to enter your Wi-Fi and city.

Button: short press = next screen. Hold 4 seconds = reset Wi-Fi and city.
